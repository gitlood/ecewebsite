4:45 am, Whanganui

Your next chapter starts at sunrise

You've spent a long career leading tamariki, kaiako and whānau. Now it's time for work that uses all of it, without the ratios. Scroll, and the sun comes up on fifteen chapters of real options, local employers, pay and people who've already made the move.

Start the day

5:00 am

The short version

An experienced ECE head teacher living in Whanganui has at least six next steps that move out of day-to-day teaching of children and can be done locally: home-based coordination, kindergarten senior teacher roles, visiting lecturing, UCOL lecturing, non-teaching centre management and consultancy. The Education Review Office (ERO), which took over ECE licensing and regulation on 1 September 2026, is a strong seventh if it can be based locally with travel.

Outside ECE, the strongest Whanganui options are Ministry of Education adviser roles, MSD case management and Corrections case management, all with local offices and pay similar to or above head teacher pay.

Most options can be tested part-time before leaving a current role. The most accessible starting points are local home-based services such as PAUA Early Childhood, UCOL Whanganui, NZTC visiting lecturer contracts and He Whānau Manaaki Kindergarten Association. A master's degree unlocks the higher-paid academic and senior roles.

Lecturing still means teaching, but of adults. When applying for anything here, ask whether the role includes ratio cover or relief teaching, because some management and coordinator roles do.

Option 1: Education Review Office

ERO is the most timely option in the sector because it now both evaluates and regulates ECE services. From 1 September 2026 it became responsible for licensing, certification, compliance monitoring, complaints, incident responses and enforcement, functions that sat with the Ministry of Education until then (ERO).

  • Review Officer (Early Childhood Education): reviews and reports on the quality of education in early childhood services. Recent listings have been in Napier and Hamilton.
  • Regulatory and licensing roles: new roles support the transfer of ECE regulation to ERO (example listing).

How your current job relates: internal evaluation, licensing compliance, Te Whāriki knowledge, writing reports for whānau, and having been on the receiving end of ERO reviews yourself.

From Whanganui: likely workable. ERO listed a Whanganui regional office in 2021 (OIA response) and has advertised some roles as remote (listing). Expect travel across the Central region, and ask the recruiter whether Whanganui can be your base before applying.

Watch out for: regional travel, report-writing load, and public service salary bands rather than the teacher pay scale.

5:45 am

Option 2: Teacher education

Teacher education offers a ladder you can start on part-time: visiting lecturer first, then lecturer once a master's is under way. You'd be teaching adults, not children.

  • Visiting Lecturer / Practicum Assessor (NZTC, Whanganui): NZTC has advertised this hybrid contract role for Whanganui specifically (listing). It asks for an ECE bachelor's degree or higher, a current practising certificate and 3–5 years' ECE experience (requirements).
  • Lecturer (UCOL Whanganui): UCOL teaches its Bachelor of Teaching (ECE) and Level 5 diploma at the Whanganui campus on Rutland Street (programme, diploma). UCOL lecturer roles accept candidates working towards a master's (example listing).
  • Blended teacher-education roles: some providers, such as Te Rito Maioha, deliver through blended learning and regional centres (opportunities); check each role's location.

How your current job relates: mentoring student and provisionally certificated teachers, appraisal against the Standards, and curriculum leadership.

From Whanganui: yes, through UCOL Whanganui and NZTC. University lecturing roles are city-based and would mean relocating.

6:00 am

Option 3: Professional learning and development

PLD facilitators coach teachers and leaders across many services, which suits someone who enjoys growing their team more than running a centre.

Playcentre Aotearoa's facilitator role designs PLD programmes, runs face-to-face and online learning for adults, and helps deliver Ministry of Education contracts (position description). Edtech companies such as Storypark employ ECE-experienced PLD trainers (Storypark PLD team).

Who employs PLD facilitators: kindergarten associations, Playcentre, large ECE providers' internal teams, private PLD companies with Ministry accreditation, and edtech providers.

How your current job relates: leading staff meetings and internal PLD, coaching conversations, and leading change in practice.

From Whanganui: possible. Playcentre employs regional facilitators for the Central North Island (team page), and He Whānau Manaaki runs PLD across its kindergartens.

6:15 am

Option 4: Centre and area management

Area or regional manager roles are the natural next rung for someone who wants to stay in operations. Large providers such as Evolve Education, BestStart and Busy Bees advertise centre, complex and area manager roles and run internal leadership programmes.

How your current job relates: budgets, occupancy, rosters, ratios, recruitment, licensing compliance and leading a team through ERO reviews.

From Whanganui: centre manager roles come up locally (Lots of Hugs Broadway listing). Area roles usually cover several towns, so ask whether one covering Manawatū–Whanganui can be based in Whanganui. In smaller centres the manager often counts in ratios, so ask how much time is spent with children before accepting.

6:30 am

More options inside ECE

Kindergarten association senior teacher or professional leader. He Whānau Manaaki Kindergarten Association has governed the Whanganui kindergartens since July 2019 (annual report), and its senior teachers support local kindergartens with curriculum, learning and appraisals (Whanganui Chronicle). Watch its vacancies and the Education Gazette. Pay follows the kindergarten collective agreement.

Home-based coordinator or visiting teacher. This option has the most Whanganui employers. A coordinator must be a qualified, registered ECE teacher and oversees up to 50 children across educators' homes (Ministry of Education). Local services include PAUA Early Childhood (ERO report), Ignite Homebased ECE (ERO report), Barnardos (Whanganui Chronicle) and Tiny Nation Taranaki Whanganui (ERO report).

Owning a service. Opening a centre or home-based service uses your operational knowledge but carries business risk and capital needs. Owners of small centres often end up covering ratios, so a home-based service (coordinating educators rather than teaching) or consultancy fits the goal of leaving teaching better. Licensing applications now go to ERO (ERO); the Ministry's guide to starting a home-based service covers staffing requirements.

Independent consultancy. Services adjusting to the new licensing criteria (in effect from 20 April 2026) and ERO's enforcement tools need help with compliance, internal evaluation and leadership coaching (timeline, Scoop).

Edtech, curriculum resources and policy. ECE software companies hire trainers and customer educators, and some roles are fully remote (Twinkl listing).

Mentor teacher (CV builder only). Formal mentoring of provisionally certificated teachers is usually done alongside a teaching role, so it doesn't get you out of teaching. Use it only as evidence of adult coaching while you apply for the roles above.

How to pick: the Ministry of Education pays best and fits most closely, but is still education. MSD and Corrections are the cleanest break from the sector with steady public service pay. Family Start keeps you working with families of young children without teaching them. Oranga Tamariki also hires casual resource workers in Whanganui at $26 an hour (listing), a way to try the work before committing to retraining.

Watch out for: shift work in prison-based roles, high caseloads in MSD and Family Start roles, and roles titled "social worker", which need registration with the Social Workers Registration Board.

Teachers who started businesses

The best example is in Whanganui itself: a qualified ECE teacher built a home-based service with hundreds of educators from a Whanganui head office.

Raewyn Overton-Stuart, PAUA Early Childhood (Whanganui). A qualified ECE teacher, she was working in another home-based service when she decided to start her own, run with her husband from a Whanganui headquarters. By 2012 PAUA had 43 staff, 31 visiting teachers and about 300 educators caring for roughly 700 children, mostly in rural central North Island areas where flexible hours suited farming families. The couple said Whanganui worked because housing was cheaper, the location was central and internet was good. (Whanganui Chronicle, 2012; PAUA listing; ERO report)

Michelle Dickie, children's furniture (Christchurch). A former preschool teacher, she started a business by accident after her husband built a toy box for her centre. The couple learned as they went, sold through Facebook and word of mouth, and she left teaching to make learning towers, climbing frames, water tables and mud kitchens full-time. (Stuff)

Chantelle Jacobs, Miss Jacobs Little Learners (Melbourne). A former teacher, she built a seven-figure business selling low-cost classroom resources to other teachers, starting from a blog and Facebook page in 2012. She tackled her lack of business background with short courses, podcasts and a business coach. (Yahoo News NZ)

Lisa Vodola, Preschool Readers (United States). After nine years as a kindergarten teacher, she built her early-reading business on the side while still teaching, then turned her programme into a training course for other teachers. (Simply Stated Media)

Lessons

  • Whanganui is not a barrier. PAUA grew from Whanganui to networks in Christchurch and Auckland.
  • Start on the side. Three of the four tested their idea before leaving their job.
  • Sell to adults, not children. Resources, furniture, training and coordinating educators all use ECE knowledge without daily teaching.
  • Get business help early. Ministry-funded services carry strict financial obligations; one Hamilton home-based operator lost her registration after company tax debts (NZ Herald). An accountant and a business mentor are worth the cost from day one.

Part-time master's programmes

All four can be studied part-time from Whanganui for roughly $11,000–$15,000 in domestic tuition. Massey and NZTC are the most practical fits: Massey is fully distance and an hour away in Palmerston North, and NZTC is the only ECE-specific option.

University of Auckland — Master of Educational Leadership (online) 2 years, one 12-week course at a time Fully online About $9,575 per 120 points (2026), roughly $14,000 for 180 points Three years' relevant experience; GPA 5.0 for direct entry Leadership and mentoring

  • Check your existing qualification. Every programme expects a Level 7 bachelor's degree or equivalent. If you hold an older Diploma of Teaching (ECE), ask about entry routes such as a postgraduate certificate first.
  • Cheaper ways in. Auckland sells single online courses for $2,130.60 each that can be credited to the master's (Auckland Online). NZTC's Postgraduate Certificate in Education (Leadership and Management) is a 60-credit stepping stone.
  • Funding. Domestic students may be eligible for a StudyLink loan; ask employers about study support.

Next steps

Start with low-risk moves that build a track record, and decide on a master's within the next year.

  1. Decide which non-teaching part of your job is most energising: leading people, coaching adults, or systems and compliance.
  2. Set job alerts on jobs.govt.nz, careers.ero.govt.nz, the Education Gazette and MSD careers.
  3. Apply for a visiting lecturer contract with NZTC to build adult-education experience.
  4. Rewrite your CV using the wording in What you already bring.
  5. Ask a senior teacher, ERO review officer, MSD case manager or lecturer for a 30-minute chat about their job.
  6. Compare the part-time master's programmes and choose one to start.
  7. Keep your practising certificate current; most options here require it or value it.

In every interview, ask how much of the role involves ratio cover or time teaching children.
